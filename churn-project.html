Case Study: Procurement Analytics

PO Spend Analysis Dashboard

Built an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze purchase order spend, supplier concentration, category performance, and department-level purchasing trends.

[ High-Res Dashboard Image or GIF Here ]

The Challenge

Procurement data is often stored in large tables, making it difficult to identify where money is being spent, which contracts drive the most spend, and which manufacturers or departments account for the largest share of purchases. The goal of this project was to turn raw PO data into a clear, interactive dashboard for faster business review and decision-making.

The Process

1. Data Preparation

I cleaned and standardized the dataset by fixing inconsistencies in manufacturer names, categories, and departments, including spacing issues, naming variations, and company suffixes. I renamed headers for clarity and created derived fields such as Month and Spend to support analysis.

2. Pivot Table Development

I built pivot tables to summarize spend by manufacturer, category, contract, department, and month. These pivots served as the reporting layer behind the dashboard and supported KPI such as Total Spend, Top Contract by Spend, Top Category by Spend, and Top Manufacturer by Spend.

3. Dashboard Design

I designed an interactive dashboard using slicers, pivot charts, and KPI cards. The dashboard allows users to filter by month and contract while monitoring monthly spend trends, department distribution, and manufacturer concentration.

Key Insights & Impact

  • The dashboard summarized $166.20M in total spend from the sample dataset.
  • Impact: Implemented an automated "High Priority" flag in Zendesk for customers with high churn-risk scores.
  • Result: Churn dropped by 12% in the first quarter following implementation.
