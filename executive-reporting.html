Case Study: EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP REPORTING

Monthly Finance Newsletter Dashboard

Built an interactive Excel newsletter for monthly reporting, designed so a data analyst can share a leadership-ready summary each month or leadership can also review the report independently.

The Challenge

Leadership needed a visual summary that could answer questions such as where money was being spent, which departments and categories were driving the largest spend, and which contracts and manufacturers had the highest impact. The goal of this project was to transform raw data into a polished monthly finance newsletter that could be shared by the data analyst on a recurring basis.

The Process

1. Data Preparation

I cleaned and structured the dataset by standardizing manufacturer names, department labels, category values, and contract information. I also derived fields such as Month, Year, Quarter, Spend, and Each Quantity to support analysis and monthly reporting.

2. Reporting Engine

I built multiple pivot tables to summarize spend by month, department, manufacturer, contract, and category. These pivot tables served as the reporting engine behind the newsletter. To support month-over-month analysis, I created a comparison view that calculates whether the selected month is higher or lower than the previous month.

3. Newsletter Design

I designed the final dashboard in an executive newsletter format within Excel using slicers, pivot tables, and charts. A month slicer was connected to the underlying reporting components so that when a user selects a month, the newsletter automatically refreshes to display updated metrics.

Key Insights & Impact

  • The dashboard summarized $166.20M in total spend for calendar year 2025 from the dataset.
  • Implants appeared as the top spend category at $72.82M.
  • ORTHO-2026-014 was the highest-spend contract.
  • CoreVista Clinical Supply represented the largest manufacturer share of spend.
  • Department analysis showed that spending was concentrated heavily in Radiology.

Skills

  • Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, XLOOKUP, helper columns, custom number formatting, dashboard design

Note

This project uses fictitious suppliers, contract identifiers, and sample procurement data for portfolio demonstration purposes.
